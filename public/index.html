Biography

About Me

Get to know the developer behind the screen and the maker behind the circuits.

Hello! I'm Akil Cyrylle Dangel, a passionate 4th-year Information Technology (IT) student at Bohol Island State University (BISU) Balilihan Campus. I am an enthusiastic software developer, game creator, and hardware maker who loves turning complex problems into clean, high-performing digital solutions.

Throughout my 4 years of IT education and hands-on projects, I have built strong practical expertise across full stack web development with Laravel and MySQL, 3D interactive gameplay mechanics in the Unity engine, desktop software systems using Java (NetBeans), and real-time embedded computing with Arduino.

As a graduating senior, I take pride in competitive excellence — proven by earning 1st Place in the 2025 MySQL Database Hackathon and being crowned 2026 Arduino Champion. I am eager to contribute my technical foundation, creativity, and dedication to industry projects and modern software engineering teams.
